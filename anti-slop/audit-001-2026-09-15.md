## Executive Summary

This audit evaluated the personal portfolio website of Adwa Najmi (`index.html`, `css/main.css`, `js/main.js`) against the 38 mandatory antislop rules and the craftsmanship standards.

While the site showcases genuine, impressive enterprise systems and real project screenshots, its visual and technical wrapper is heavily saturated with the stereotypical AI "cyberpunk/hacker" template tropes: cyan-to-purple neon gradients, floating glow blobs, an animated canvas grid, a fake terminal window, repeated `//` syntax prefixes, and pervasive em dashes (`—`). More critically, several **Hard Gate accessibility failures** were discovered: severe low-contrast text failing WCAG AA (down to 1.94:1), completely unkeyboardable project cards (`<div onclick>`), and sub-44px touch targets.

Per **Mode 2 protocol**, all findings are numbered below by priority tier. **No source code has been altered.** Please review the list and specify which finding numbers you approve for remediation.

## Findings by Priority

### Tier 1: Hard Gate (Priority: HIGH)

Breaking any of these rules is an immediate failure of honesty, accessibility, or core function.

#### [Finding 01] Forbidden Em Dash (`—`) in UI Text & Headings
- **Rule Violated:** `R-02 — Copywriting` (Hard Gate)
- **Priority:** HIGH
- **Reason:** Antislop strictly forbids the em dash character (`—`) in user-facing copy. It is one of the most recognizable AI writing tells.
- **Occurrences (21 instances in `index.html`):**
  - Lines 9, 10, 18, 25: `<title>` and `<meta>` tags (`Adwa Najmi — Software Engineer & Laravel Developer`)
  - Line 184: About text (`...challenging technical environments — specializing in...`)
  - Lines 250, 256, 629: Bably heading (`Bably — Smart Baby Care...`)
  - Lines 265, 674: Kira+ heading (`Kira+ — Tax Relief...`)
  - Lines 286, 721: EMS v2 HR Training heading (`EMS v2 — HR Training...`)
  - Lines 301, 767: EMS v2 Driver Assessment heading (`EMS v2 — Driver Assessment...`)
  - Lines 316, 809: EMS v2 Employee Toolkit heading (`EMS v2 — Employee Toolkit...`)
  - Lines 484, 502, 520, 536: Journey timeline dates (`Mar 2023 — Present`, `Jan 2022 — Feb 2023`, etc.)
  - Line 700: Kira+ Year (`2025 — 2026`)
  - Also present in `js/main.js:2, 302` and `css/main.css:2`.
- **Recommended Remediation:** Replace em dashes with appropriate punctuation (hyphens `-`, en dashes `–`, colons `:`, commas, or prepositions like `to` for date ranges).

#### [Finding 02] Severe Text Contrast Failures Failing WCAG AA Standards
- **Rule Violated:** `R-25 — Color Contrast` (Hard Gate) & `antislop-human`
- **Priority:** HIGH
- **Reason:** All normal text must maintain at least a 4.5:1 contrast ratio against its background. Multiple text styles fail drastically, excluding low-vision readers.
- **Measured Ratios (via `contrast-check.py`):**
  - `--text-muted: rgba(232, 232, 240, 0.25)` on `--bg-deep: #08080f` yields effective color `#404047` with contrast ratio **1.94:1** (FAIL: requires >= 4.5:1). Used in 13 locations including `.work-tech` (card footer tags, `css/main.css:931`), dates, and modal notes.
  - `.label-num` in `.section-label.light` (`css/main.css:189`) with `opacity: 0.5` on magenta yields `#831a43` on `#08080f` with contrast ratio **2.09:1** (FAIL for 12px text).
  - `.label-text` in `.section-label.light` (`css/main.css:193`) with `opacity: 0.8` on magenta yields `#cd2563` on `#08080f` with contrast ratio **3.84:1** (FAIL for 12px text).
  - `.label-num` in default `.section-label` with `opacity: 0.5` on cyan yields `#047c87` on `#08080f` with contrast ratio **4.03:1** (FAIL for 12px text).
- **Recommended Remediation:** Increase text opacity/lightness for muted text to achieve a minimum 4.5:1 ratio (e.g. `#8e8e99` or higher), and remove excessive opacity dampening on colored labels.

#### [Finding 03] Work Cards Inaccessible to Keyboard Navigation & Missing Focus Indicators
- **Rule Violated:** `R-32 — Keyboard Accessibility` (Hard Gate) & `C-4 — Resilience`
- **Priority:** HIGH
- **Reason:** Interactive elements must be navigable via `Tab` and activatable via `Enter`/`Space`. `css/main.css` has zero focus indicator styles.
- **Evidence:**
  - All 11 project cards in `index.html:251-458` are coded as `<div class="work-card" onclick="openModal(...)">`. Because they are plain `<div>` elements without `tabindex="0"`, `role="button"`, or keyboard listeners, keyboard-only users cannot discover or open project case study modals.
  - No `:focus` or `:focus-visible` styles exist in `css/main.css`, leaving interactive elements without visible focus outlines.
- **Recommended Remediation:** Convert cards to accessible interactive elements (or add `role="button"`, `tabindex="0"`, and `keydown` Enter/Space handler in JS), and add clear, high-contrast `:focus-visible` rings across all interactive controls.

#### [Finding 04] Dead / Ghost External Social Links
- **Rule Violated:** `R-24 — Navigation`, `R-26 — Interactive Elements`, `R-38 — Real Content` (Hard Gate)
- **Priority:** HIGH
- **Reason:** All navigation and external links must lead to real, working destinations, not placeholder landing pages.
- **Evidence:**
  - Hero social links (`index.html:123`): `<a href="https://www.linkedin.com/" target="_blank" aria-label="LinkedIn">`
  - Contact section (`index.html:590`): `<a href="https://www.linkedin.com/" target="_blank" class="contact-link">`
  - Both point to the root domain of LinkedIn rather than Adwa's personal profile URL.
- **Recommended Remediation:** Update with Adwa's actual LinkedIn profile URL, or remove the link if no public profile is available.

#### [Finding 05] Mobile Touch Targets Below 44px Standard
- **Rule Violated:** `R-03 — Mobile Responsiveness` (Hard Gate) & `antislop-layoutmobile`
- **Priority:** HIGH
- **Reason:** Touch controls must meet the 44px minimum tap target boundary to be comfortably usable on mobile devices.
- **Evidence:**
  - `.work-arrow` button is `3.2rem` = 32px by 32px (`css/main.css:934`), 12px below the 44px requirement.
  - `.filter-btn` has `padding: 0.8rem 1.8rem; font-size: 1.2rem;` totaling ~36px height (`css/main.css:1679`).
- **Recommended Remediation:** Expand touch boundaries to a minimum of 44px × 44px using padding or min-height/min-width constraints.

#### [Finding 11] Repetitive Button Arrows (`→`) on Every Card
- **Rule Violated:** `R-08 — Button Arrows`
- **Priority:** MEDIUM
- **Reason:** Right arrows (`<i class="fas fa-arrow-right"></i>`) are stamped on every work card footer and the hero CTA button as pure ornamentation.
- **Location:** `index.html:113`, `index.html:260, 275, 290, 305, 320, 335, 350, 365, 380, 395, 411, 426, 441, 456`.
- **Recommended Remediation:** Remove the circular arrow button from cards where the entire card is clickable; keep arrows only where indicating an explicit forward external transition.

#### [Finding 12] Unchecked Infinite Motion & Missing `prefers-reduced-motion`
- **Rule Violated:** `R-19 — Animations` & `antislop-human`
- **Priority:** MEDIUM
- **Reason:** Continuous 60fps canvas repaints, 20-second blob floating animations, and slideshow intervals run continuously without accommodating users with vestibular disorders.
- **Location:** `css/main.css`, `js/main.js`.
- **Recommended Remediation:** Add `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` in CSS and check `window.matchMedia('(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)')` in JS to pause canvas and keyframe loops.

#### [Finding 13] AI Capsule Badge Template ("Open to Opportunities")

- **Priority:** MEDIUM
- **Reason:** Capsule pill + thin border + glowing pulsing dot is the textbook AI badge format.
- **Location:** `index.html:80-83`, `css/main.css:327-366`.
- **Recommended Remediation:** Simplify to a tasteful, calm status indicator without glowing pill borders and exaggerated dot pulsing.
